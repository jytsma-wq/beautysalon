## Uncontrolled Domain Policy

Because the owner does not control `silkbeauty.ge`:

- Do not attempt DNS, redirect, canonical, Search Console, hosting, or content
  changes on that domain.
- Do not describe it as an owned legacy site.
- Do not include it in `sameAs`, citations, social profiles, GBP, Bing, or
  booking links.
- Do not submit a Google Change of Address request; ownership of both properties
  is required and the domains are not an approved migration pair.
- Do not file an impersonation, trademark, hosting-abuse, or legal complaint
  without owner-provided evidence and a separate explicit decision.

The domain should be monitored as an uncontrolled brand/entity collision. Its
existence can divide branded and Georgian search signals, but first-party code
cannot safely redirect or canonicalize it.

## Next No-Cost Actions

1. Keep GBP, Search Console, social profiles, Salonly, Bing, and other
   owner-controlled citations linked only to `silkbeautysalon.online`.
2. Correct stale citation text and duplicate social identities only after
   ownership is confirmed.
3. Build authority through real Google reviews, real salon photos, relevant

4. Monitor branded and Georgian queries for `.online` versus `.ge` selection.
5. If the owner believes `.ge` is unauthorized or misleading, collect proof of
